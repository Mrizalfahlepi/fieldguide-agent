INSTRUCTIONS FOR USING 
Miniature Circuit Breaker 
 
Series 
MCB manufactured according to 
IEC60898-1 standard 
 
 
 
 
 Be sure to read the manual and follow all safety precautions before using the product. 
 This manual should be given to the person who will actually use the product and be responsible for 
its maintenance. 
 
 1. Key Features 
 
Rated Voltage: 240/415V 
 
Rated Current: 6, 10, 16, 20, 25, 32, 40, 50, 63A 
 
Breaking Capacity: Icu=Ics =6kA or Icu=10kA, Ics=7.5kA 
 
Type: B, C, D curve 
 
Applications: Residential, commercial, industrial 
 
2. Installation 
1. 
Preparation: 
o 
Ensure power supply is disconnected before installation. 
o 
Verify the MCB rating matches the circuit requirements. 
2. 
Mounting: 
o 
Place the MCB on the DIN rail in the distribution board. 
o 
Ensure it is securely fixed and aligned. 
3. 
Wiring: 
o 
Connect the incoming (supply) wires to the top terminals and the outgoing (load) wires 
to the bottom terminals. 
o 
Tighten screws securely to ensure proper electrical contact. 
 
3. Operation 
1. 
Switching On: 
o 
Push the toggle lever to the "ON" position to power the circuit. 
2. 
Switching Off: 
o 
Push the toggle lever to the "OFF" position to isolate the circuit. 
3. 
Tripping: 
o 
The MCB will trip automatically during overcurrent or short-circuit conditions. 
o 
Inspect and resolve the fault before resetting. 
 
4. Safety Guidelines 
 
Always ensure the circuit is de-energized before working on the MCB. 
 
Use an MCB within its specified voltage and current limits. 
 
Avoid exposure to moisture, dust, or corrosive environments. 
 
5. Troubleshooting 
Issue 
Possible Cause 
Solution 
MCB does not power on 
Loose or incorrect wiring 
Check and tighten connections 
Frequent tripping 
Overload or short circuit 
Reduce load, inspect circuit 
Lever stuck 
Internal damage 
Replace the MCB 
 
6. Maintenance 
 
Periodically inspect for signs of wear, overheating, or damage. 
 
Test the MCB trip mechanism by pressing the test button (if available).
